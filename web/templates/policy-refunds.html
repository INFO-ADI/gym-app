Free trial

Every gym starts with a free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial, and you can stop using the app any time without paying.

Cancelling a plan

You can stop auto-pay at any time from the app (More → App plan → Stop auto-pay) or by messaging us. Cancelling stops future charges. The app keeps working until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Refunds

Because every gym can try the full app free before paying, plan payments are generally not refunded once a period has started. We do refund in these cases:

  • You were charged twice for the same period, or charged after you had cancelled auto-pay.
  • A technical problem on our side kept the app from working for most of a paid period.
  • You ask within 7 days of your first ever paid month or year and have not used the app since that payment.

To ask for a refund, contact us with your gym name and the payment date. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Gym members' fees

Fees that members pay to their gym go directly to the gym. Refunds of those fees are handled by the gym, not by {{SITE}}.
