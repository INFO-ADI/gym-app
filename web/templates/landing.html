Questions gym owners ask

Do members need to download anything from the Play Store?

No. They open your gym's link and tap "Add to Home screen". It then opens like any other app, on Android and iPhone.

Can other gyms see my members or income?

No. Each gym's data is kept separate in the database, and only your login can open it.

I have 300 members in a register. Do I type them all?

No. Send us the list in any form, Excel or a photo of the register, and we add them for you, free.

What happens after the free month?

Pay monthly or yearly by UPI to keep going. If you stop, the app pauses and your data stays saved for when you come back.

Do I need a computer?

No. Everything works on your phone. A computer works too if you prefer a bigger screen.
