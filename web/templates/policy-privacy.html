This policy explains what information {{SITE}} ({{DOMAIN}}) keeps and how it is used.

What we keep

Gym owners: name, email address, phone number, gym details and payment records for the {{SITE}} plan.
Gym members (entered by their gym): name, phone number, membership, fee and attendance records, and optional details such as weight or diet plan.
Website visitors who book a demo: name, gym name, phone number and city.

How it is used

Only to run the app: show each gym its own members and payments, send reminders, process plan payments and give support. We do not sell or rent personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Each gym's data is kept separate and is visible only to that gym's owner logins and, for their own records, its members. Our platform administrators can see gym account and payment details to provide support and billing.

Payments

Card and UPI auto-pay details are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store card numbers or UPI PINs. Razorpay's privacy policy applies to the payment step.

Storage and security

Data is stored with our hosting and database providers on secure servers, sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and protected by passwords and access rules that keep gyms apart.

Your choices

A gym can download its data at any time from the app. To correct or delete data, gym members should contact their gym; gym owners can contact us. When a gym account is deleted, its data is deleted with it.

Contact

For any privacy question, see our contact page.
